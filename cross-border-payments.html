cross-border payments — your questions

What counts as a cross-border payment?

A cross-border payment is any transaction where the payer and payee are in different countries — often crossing currencies and multiple banks (correspondent banking). For high-risk businesses the challenge is finding regulated providers that accept the sector and settle quickly without punitive FX. That's exactly what we place.

How do you get better cross-border rates and service?

With 12+ years in high-risk payments and direct relationships with tier-1 and tier-2 licensed institutions, we negotiate transparent FX and priority service on your behalf, and route your flow to the corridor and provider that prices it best — an advantage a single business rarely gets approaching banks cold.

Do you work with regulated, licensed providers only?

Yes. Our cross-border network is built on licensed payment institutions, EMIs and banking partners operating under recognised regulatory frameworks, with proper AML and sanctions screening. You remain responsible for your own licensing; we match you to compliant counterparties.

Can you help with correspondent banking access?

Yes. Correspondent banking is the backbone of cross-border settlement, and de-risking has made it hard to access. We introduce you to providers and networks with the correspondent relationships and local rails your corridors require.

Which regions and currencies do you cover?

We work globally, matching providers to the specific corridors and currencies your business needs — including harder-to-serve emerging markets. Tell us your flows and we'll map the regulated options.
