Why it's high-risk

Why serious size trades OTC, not on-exchange

Above a certain ticket, order books slip against you and settlement gets messy. OTC solves both — if you can access the right desks.

  • Large orders cause slippage and move thin exchange order books
  • Public trades leak intent and invite front-running
  • High-volume and high-risk clients get declined by retail venues
  • Cross-asset (crypto↔fiat) settlement needs trusted counterparties
  • Best pricing sits behind relationships, not sign-up forms

OTC payments — your questions

What is an OTC desk?

An OTC (over-the-counter) desk lets you trade large volumes of crypto or FX directly with a counterparty instead of on a public exchange order book. You get a firm quote for the whole size at once, avoiding the slippage and market impact that large on-exchange orders cause. We connect you to vetted desks and, through 12+ years of relationships, negotiate better pricing and service than approaching them cold.

How much better are OTC rates through Pay-Team?

Because we bring aggregated flow and long-standing relationships with tier-1 and tier-2 desks, we routinely secure tighter spreads and priority service than a single client walking in alone. Exact improvement depends on asset, size and frequency, but relationship pricing consistently beats retail.

What's the minimum size for OTC trading?

OTC is designed for large tickets — typically where an on-exchange order would move the market. Minimums vary by desk and asset. Tell us your typical size and we'll match you to desks whose thresholds and liquidity fit.

Do you cover both crypto and FX OTC?

Yes. We place crypto OTC (BTC, ETH, stablecoins and more) and FX OTC (spot, forwards, NDFs across major and exotic pairs), including fiat-to-crypto conversion at size through trusted counterparties.

Is OTC trading regulated?

Reputable OTC desks operate under the licensing and AML frameworks of their jurisdictions, and we only introduce vetted, compliant providers. You'll complete the desk's KYC/AML onboarding. We match you to counterparties appropriate to your regulatory profile; nothing here is legal or investment advice.
