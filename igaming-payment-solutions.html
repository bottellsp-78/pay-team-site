Why iGaming is high-risk payment processing

Gambling is one of the most tightly controlled payment verticals in the world — and one of the most lucrative when your rails work.

  • Strict, jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction gambling licensing and regulation
  • High deposit/withdrawal frequency and chargeback exposure
  • Card scheme rules and MCC 7995 gambling coding requirements
  • Responsible-gambling, AML and player-protection obligations
  • Region-locking and blocked payment methods in many markets

Answers

iGaming payments — your questions

Do I need a gambling licence to get an iGaming merchant account?

In almost all cases, yes. Legitimate acquirers require a valid gambling licence for the markets you serve. We match your specific licence — whether a regulated national licence or an offshore one — to acquirers who accept that licence type and jurisdiction.

Which deposit methods can you set up for players?

Beyond cards, we place local bank transfers, open banking, e-wallets, prepaid vouchers and region-specific APMs. Local methods often out-convert cards in many iGaming markets, so a mix is usually optimal.

How fast can operators pay out winnings?

We prioritise providers offering instant or same-day payout rails, because withdrawal speed is one of the biggest drivers of player retention and reputation in iGaming.

Can you help offshore-licensed casinos and sportsbooks?

Yes. Much of our acquiring network supports offshore gambling licences alongside regulated markets, so newer and international operators can still access reliable deposits and payouts.

How do you protect against chargebacks and keep our MIDs safe?

We combine correctly-coded gambling MIDs, multi-acquirer orchestration, 3D Secure and AML/RG tooling to keep chargebacks under scheme thresholds and reduce the risk of account reviews or closures.
