Before you reach out

Does it cost anything to contact you?

No. Getting matched and introduced is completely free for merchants — we're paid by the providers we place business with.

What information should I have ready?

Your vertical, expected monthly card volume, licensing/jurisdiction and what you need first (acquiring, APMs, banking or crypto settlement). The more complete your picture, the faster we can match you.

How quickly will I hear back?

WhatsApp and Telegram are fastest — often within hours during business time. Form and email enquiries are typically answered same or next business day, with provider introductions usually inside 24–72 hours.

Do you work with startups and newly-licensed businesses?

Yes. A large part of our network is built for newly-incorporated and offshore-licensed merchants that traditional banks decline.
