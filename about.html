How We Work

We're the placement desk between high-risk merchants and the providers that approve them.

For 12+ years Pay-Team has been the independent placement desk for high-risk businesses. We don't hold your funds or process your payments. We do something more valuable: through high-level contacts built across the industry, we know which acquirers, PSPs, EMIs, OTC desks and cross-border providers actually approve FX, crypto, iGaming and adult businesses — and we secure better rates and service from tier-1 and tier-2 providers than you'd get alone.

The process

What working with us looks like

Discovery

You share vertical, volume, licensing, jurisdictions and what you need first. Two minutes on the form or a chat on Telegram/WhatsApp.

Matching
