03 Bolivia in detail

What you need to know.

Why we will not promise you T+0 in Bolivia

Bolivia has been dealing with a severe and sustained shortage of foreign currency. That shortage is the reason the government has been publicly examining whether to bring Tether’s USDT into the national payment system — a step no country takes when hard currency is readily available.

On top of that, Bolivia has been on the FATF list of jurisdictions under increased monitoring since June 2025, having made a high-level political commitment to strengthen its AML and counter-terrorist-financing regime. Grey-list status means correspondent banks apply enhanced scrutiny, and enhanced scrutiny takes time.

Anyone quoting you guaranteed same-day settlement into Bolivia on the same terms as Brazil is either not doing it properly, or not doing it at all. We would rather tell you that before you commit a shipment to it.

What we can do

Bolivia is workable when the trade is genuine, the documentation is complete and the timeline is realistic. We place clients with counterparties that hold real Bolivian correspondent capability rather than claiming coverage they cannot execute, and we tell you upfront what the corridor will cost in both money and days.

If Bolivia is one leg of a wider Latin American footprint, it is usually better handled as part of a regional structure. We have placed global automotive manufacturers and household-name online businesses into these corridors. Same rails, same desks, same compliance file.

Questions

Straight answers.

Why is Bolivia harder than Brazil or Colombia?

Two reasons. Bolivia has an acute shortage of US dollars, which constrains FX availability and pricing. And it has been on the FATF list of jurisdictions under increased monitoring since June 2025, so correspondent banks apply enhanced due diligence, which adds time.

Can you guarantee same-day settlement to Bolivia?

No, and we would be cautious of anyone who does. Timing depends on FX availability and compliance review. We quote realistic timelines for your specific flow rather than a headline number.

Is Bolivia on the FATF grey list?

Yes. Bolivia has been under FATF increased monitoring since June 2025. It is one of only two Latin American jurisdictions currently listed, alongside Venezuela.

Is Bolivia adopting stablecoins for payments?

The government has publicly considered integrating Tether’s USDT into the national payment system in response to the dollar shortage. It is a live policy discussion rather than a settled framework, and we would not build a client corridor on it today.
