03 Why this is a placement problem

Coverage claimed is not coverage delivered.

Every country in this list works differently

Brazil clears domestically in seconds over Pix but is about to tighten what eFX providers may use for settlement. Colombia built Bre-B into a fully interoperable instant system that has already cleared over 500 million transactions. Argentina removed most of its exchange controls in April 2025 and became a genuinely different market. Peru went wallet-first and interoperable. Bolivia is short of dollars and on the FATF grey list.

A provider that is excellent in Brazil is frequently mediocre in Peru and absent in Bolivia. The regional coverage map on a sales deck is not the same as a bank that will actually take your file.

Questions

Straight answers.

Which Latin American countries do you cover?

Brazil, Argentina, Colombia, Peru and Bolivia, for payments in both directions. We place both importers and exporters, and we cover goods, services and platform payouts.

Can you settle same day?

In Brazil, Colombia and Peru, same-day settlement is routine within the FX cut-off, because the domestic rails clear in seconds. Argentina is achievable on a clean file since the 2025 liberalisation. Bolivia is the exception and we quote it case by case.

Do I need a local entity in each country?

No. We place you with licensed institutions that settle domestically in local currency while you fund in your own, with the FX contract and documentation held in your name.

Do you use stablecoins to move the money?

No. These are regulated bank and licensed-FX corridors. Brazil is a good example of why that matters: Resolution BCB 561 stops eFX providers settling cross-border payments in crypto or stablecoins from October 2026.

What does it cost to get matched?

Nothing. Our partners pay us, so the shortlist and the introductions are free to you.
