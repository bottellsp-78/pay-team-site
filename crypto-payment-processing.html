Why it's high-risk

Why crypto is a high-risk payments vertical

Crypto businesses are debanked constantly. The right partners treat compliance as a feature, not a reason to decline.

  • AML/KYC and travel-rule obligations make banks cautious
  • Volatility and irreversibility raise perceived settlement risk
  • Fragmented, fast-moving global regulation (MiCA, VASP regimes and more)
  • Fraud and money-laundering exposure requires strong monitoring
  • Reputational caution and blanket de-risking of the sector by banks

Answers

crypto payments — your questions

Can you get banking for a crypto exchange or VASP?

Yes — crypto banking is one of our most-requested placements. We work with EMIs and banks that actively serve licensed virtual-asset service providers, offering dedicated IBANs and safeguarding rather than the blanket de-risking most high-street banks apply.

What's the difference between a crypto gateway and crypto settlement?

A crypto payment gateway lets your customers pay you in crypto. Crypto settlement lets a business (even a fiat one) receive its revenue in stablecoins or crypto. We place both, and often combine them so you accept cards and settle in USDT/USDC.

Do you support fiat on-ramp and off-ramp?

Yes. We place card-to-crypto on-ramps and crypto-to-bank off-ramps for exchanges, wallets and apps, with the KYC/AML controls acquirers and regulators expect.

Is crypto payment processing legal in my market?

Crypto regulation varies widely by jurisdiction — from MiCA in the EU to VASP registration regimes elsewhere. We match you to providers licensed for your markets, but you're responsible for holding the correct registrations. Nothing here is legal advice.

How do you reduce fraud in crypto flows?

We place gateways and acquirers with chain-analytics, device fingerprinting, travel-rule and transaction-monitoring tooling, so you meet AML obligations while keeping approval rates high.
